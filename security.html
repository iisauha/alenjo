Security

Last updated November 20, 2026

Alenjo shows sensitive financial data, so it is built to see as little as possible and keep what it does see locked to you.

Your bank login

You sign in to your bank inside Plaid's own window. Your bank username and password go to Plaid and your bank, never to Alenjo. Alenjo receives a read-only key that can fetch balances and transactions. It cannot move money, pay bills or change anything at your bank.

Signing in

  • Email and password through Supabase Auth. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never as plain text.
  • Face ID uses a passkey: your device keeps the private key and Alenjo only stores the public half. Alenjo never receives biometric data.
  • With Face ID set up, Alenjo locks after 1, 5 or 15 minutes away (your choice) and one Face ID check unlocks it. Without Face ID, you're signed out instead.
  • New accounts need an invite code that is checked on the server.

Your data in storage

  • Every database table uses row-level security, so one user can never read another user's rows, even if the app had a bug.
  • Account names, balances, last four digits and Plaid connection keys are encrypted inside the database on top of Supabase's disk encryption.
  • Plaid connection keys are only used inside server functions and are never sent to your browser.

Data in transit

Everything loads over HTTPS: the app itself, the database, Plaid, logos and fonts. There are no unencrypted connections.

Server functions

A few Supabase Edge Functions handle the sensitive work: exchanging Plaid's one-time token for a connection key, syncing transactions and balances, and removing a bank when you disconnect it. Each one checks that you're signed in and only touches your own data.

In the app

  • Text from banks and from you is escaped before it's shown, to block script injection.
  • No financial data is kept in your browser's storage, only settings.
  • Card design uploads are checked for explicit content on your device before they are saved.

What you can do

  • Use a unique password for Alenjo and keep it in your device's password manager.
  • Disconnect banks you no longer use in Settings → Banks.
  • Keep your phone and laptop locked when you're not using them.
