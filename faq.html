FAQ

Last updated November 20, 2026

The basics

What is Alenjo?
A private personal finance app. It connects to your banks through Plaid and shows your balances, spending and credit cards in one place.
Can anyone sign up?
No. Alenjo is private and new accounts need an invite code.
Does it work on my phone and laptop?
Yes. It adapts to each screen: tabs at the bottom on a phone, a sidebar on a laptop. On iPhone, open it in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen to use it like an app.
How do I switch between light and dark mode?
Settings → Appearance. System follows your device; Light and Dark lock it.

Signing in

How does Face ID work?
Tap the Face ID icon in the password field (or the field itself) and your device offers your saved password with Face ID. To have it ask as soon as the app opens, turn on Settings → Security → Ask for Face ID on open.
Why do I have to sign in so often?
For safety, Alenjo signs you out whenever the app reloads and after 1 minute without activity.
What does “Not you?” do?
It forgets the email and name saved on this device so someone else can sign in.
I forgot my password.
Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and a reset link is emailed to you.

Banks

How do I connect a bank?
Settings → Banks → Connect a bank, or the Connect button on Home. Plaid opens and you sign in to your bank there.
Can Alenjo move my money?
No. The connection is read-only.
I got a new card at a bank I already connected.
Settings → Banks → Manage on that bank, then select the new card. No need to reconnect.
How do I disconnect a bank?
Settings → Banks → Disconnect. This deletes that bank’s accounts and transactions from Alenjo right away.
How fresh is my data?
Alenjo syncs when you open it (at most every few minutes). Banks usually update once or twice a day, so new purchases can take a few hours to show up.
What does “Include pending charges” do?
With it on, balances count purchases that haven’t cleared yet, so you see what you can actually spend. With it off, you see posted balances that match your bank statement.

Activity

What does Ignore do?
It leaves the transaction in your list but removes it from spending totals. Handy for transfers between your own accounts.
What about Reimbursed?
Same as Ignore, but tagged “Reimbursed” for purchases someone paid you back for in full.
How does splitting work?
Split evenly between people, or enter just your share. Only your share counts toward spending.
Can I change a category or rename a transaction?
Yes. Tap the transaction and choose Change Category or Edit. Your changes stick when new data syncs.
Can I add a cash purchase?
Yes. Tap + Add on the Activity tab.
Can I export my transactions?
Yes. Tap CSV on the Activity tab to download the selected month.

Cards and investments

How do custom card designs work?
Settings → Card designs → Upload a screenshot from Apple Wallet or a photo of your card, crop it, then tap an account on Home to pick its design.
How do I track a sign-up bonus?
Tap a credit card’s balance on Home, set the spending goal and time window, and Alenjo tracks your progress.
Why are some card details missing?
Some banks don’t share APR, minimum payment or due dates through Plaid. Alenjo shows them whenever the bank provides them.
What investment data is shown?
Holdings (ticker, shares, value) from your brokerage, included in your net worth on Home.

Privacy

Is my data sold or tracked?
No. There are no ads, no analytics and no cookies. See Privacy and Security for the details.
Can I delete everything?
Disconnecting a bank deletes its data right away. To delete your whole account, email the address below.
