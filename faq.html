Getting started

What is Alenjo?
A private app that pulls your bank, card and investment accounts in through Plaid so you can see balances and spending in one place.
Can anyone sign up?
No. Sign-ups are closed, and new accounts need an invite code.
Does it work on phone and laptop?
Yes. Under 900px wide you get tabs at the bottom; wider screens get a sidebar. On iPhone, open alenjo.com in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
How do I get the newest version?
When an update is out, the app shows a full-screen “new version” message. Tap Update now; your settings carry over.

Signing in

How do I set up Face ID?
Sign in with your password, then Settings → Face ID & lock → Set up. After that, tap the Face ID icon in the password box. It also works on your Mac and iPad through iCloud Keychain.
Why does it lock itself?
After 1, 5 or 15 minutes away (Settings → Face ID & lock → Auto-lock). With Face ID it unlocks right where you were. Without Face ID you sign in again.
What does “Not you?” do?
Forgets the email and name saved on that device.
I forgot my password.
Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and a reset link is emailed to you.

Home

What is “Available”?
Cash in your checking accounts minus what you owe on credit cards. Assets and Liabilities beside it add in savings and investments.
What does tapping the big number do?
Switches between counting pending charges and posted-only balances. Same as Settings → Include pending charges.
How do I read the chart?
Drag across it to see any day; the numbers above change to that day. 1W, 1M, 3M and 1Y change the range.
How do I reorder or hide accounts?
Settings → Home screen. Open a group, drag ≡ to reorder, tap the eye to hide. Tap an account on Home to leave it out of totals or add a savings APY.

Transactions

Which date is used?
The day the card was swiped. Tap a transaction to see the swipe and post dates. If the bank didn’t share a swipe date, the post date is used and it says so.
What do the colors mean?
Red is money out, green is money in, orange is still pending. Pending turns red or green once the bank posts it.
Rent posted on the 1st but belongs to last month.
Tap it, then the date button, and pick a date in the right month. It counts there for totals and exports. Reset puts it back.
How do I rename one or change the amount?
Tap the ✎ next to the name or the amount. The tag shows Renamed or Adjusted; the bank’s original is kept.
How does splitting work?
Split › → tap 2, 3, 4 or type a number of people, or type what you owe or what you got back and the other fills in. Only your share counts toward spending.
Can I make my own categories?
Yes. Category › → type a name → Add. Tap Edit there to rename or delete. Deleting a category moves its transactions to Uncategorized.
A logo is missing or wrong.
Tap the logo in the transaction and search for the store. Your pick applies to every transaction from that store.
What do Ignore and Reimbursed do?
Both keep the transaction but take it out of spending. Use Reimbursed when someone paid you back in full.
A purchase is missing.
Tap + Add on Activity, for cash or anything your bank didn’t pick up.
Can I export?
Yes. Export as CSV at the bottom of the Activity list exports the month you’re viewing, with notes.

Banks

How fresh is my data?
Alenjo syncs when you open it, at most every few minutes. Plaid checks your bank on its own schedule, usually a few times a day, so a new purchase can take a few hours.
A bank says “needs attention”.
The bank wants you to sign in again (password change, expired permission). Tap Reconnect. It’s the same connection, so your history and edits stay.
New card at a bank I already connected?
Settings → Bank connections → Manage on that bank, then select the new card.
Can Alenjo move my money?
No. The connection is read-only.
Why are some card details missing?
Some banks don’t share APR, minimum payment or due date through Plaid.

Your data

Can I see the code?
Yes. The app’s code is public at github.com/iisauha/alenjo. The server functions that talk to Plaid run on Supabase.
Is my data sold or used for ads?
No. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking scripts. See Privacy and Security for exactly what’s stored and who sees it.
How do I delete everything?
Settings → Delete account. Every bank is disconnected at Plaid, and your data, photos and sign-in are deleted.
