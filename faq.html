FAQ

The basics

What is Alenjo?
A private finance app. It connects to your banks through Plaid and shows balances, spending and credit cards in one place.
Can anyone sign up?
No. New accounts need an invite code.
Phone or laptop?
Both. It adapts to the screen: tabs at the bottom on a phone, a sidebar on a laptop. On iPhone, open it in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
Light or dark mode?
Settings → Appearance. System follows your device.

Signing in

How does Face ID work?
Set it up in Settings → Security → Face ID sign-in, then tap the Face ID icon in the password box. It syncs to your Mac and iPad through iCloud Keychain.
Why does Alenjo lock itself?
So nobody can see your money if you leave your phone unlocked. Pick 1, 5 or 15 minutes in Settings → Security.
What does “Not you?” do?
Forgets the saved email and name on this device.
I forgot my password.
Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.

Home

What is “Available”?
Cash in your checking accounts minus what you owe on credit cards. Assets and Liabilities beside it show the bigger picture.
How do I read the chart?
Drag your finger across it to see any day. The numbers above change to that day. Tap 1W, 1M, 3M or 1Y to zoom.
Can I hide or reorder accounts?
Tap an account to hide it or leave it out of totals. To reorder, drag the ≡ handle in Settings → Accounts.
How fresh is my data?
Alenjo syncs when you open it, at most every few minutes. Banks usually update once or twice a day.

Activity

Which date does a transaction use?
The day the card was swiped. Tap a transaction to see both the swipe date and the posted date.
Rent posted on the 1st but belongs to last month.
Tap it and use ‹ Oct or Dec › under “Counts in”. It moves to that month for totals and exports. Undo is one tap.
Can I add a note?
Yes. Tap a transaction and type in the note box at the top. Notes show in the list and are searchable.
What do Ignore and Reimbursed do?
Both keep the transaction but take it out of spending. Reimbursed is for things someone paid you back for.
How does splitting work?
Split evenly or enter just your share. Only your share counts toward spending.
A purchase is missing.
Tap + Add on Activity to add it yourself, for cash or anything your bank didn’t pick up.
Can I export?
Yes. Export as CSV is at the bottom of the Activity list.

Banks and cards

How do I connect a bank?
Settings → Banks → Connect a bank. Plaid opens and you sign in there.
Can Alenjo move my money?
No. The connection is read-only.
New card at a bank I already connected?
Settings → Banks → Manage, then select the new card.
How do card designs work?
Upload a photo of the card in Settings → Card designs, then tap the account on Home to pick it.
Why are some card details missing?
Some banks don’t share APR, minimum payment or due dates through Plaid.

Privacy

Is my data sold or tracked?
No. No ads, analytics or cookies. See Privacy and Security.
Can I delete everything?
Yes. Settings → Delete account disconnects every bank and permanently deletes all your data, photos and sign-in. Disconnecting one bank deletes just that bank’s data.
