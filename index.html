Billing

Every connected bank costs moneyPlaid charges a monthly fee for each bank you connect, for as long as it stays connected. Nothing here is free. What matters is what adds to that fee.

Adds nothing once a bank is connected

  • Reconnect. When a bank asks you to sign in again. It’s the same connection, so the fee stays the same.
  • Syncing. New transactions, balances and due dates come in as part of the monthly fee.

Manage: adding accounts at a connected bank

Adding accounts costs nothing, with two exceptionsEach bank pays once for cards and loans and once for investments. Only the first of each kind at a bank adds to the bill.
  • Checking and savings: nothing extra.
  • First card or loan at that bank: +$0.20 a month. More cards or loans there add nothing.
  • First investment account at that bank: +$0.18 a month. More investment accounts there add nothing.

Adds to the bill

  • A new bank. $0.30 a month, plus $0.20 if it has cards or loans, plus $0.18 if it has investments.
  • The first card, loan or investment account at a bank, as above.
  • Disconnecting a bank, then adding it back. Plaid treats it as a brand new bank and bills both that month. Use Reconnect or Manage instead.

Good to know

  • Months are billed whole. A bank connected for even one day is billed for that month, so disconnecting partway through doesn’t save it.
  • Disconnect stops the fee from next month.
  • A bank that needs reconnecting is still billed until you reconnect or disconnect it.

From the founder

Isaiah AguilarFounder · environmental engineer

Hi, I’m Isaiah. I’m an environmental engineer who’s a little obsessed with personal finance.

I kept getting frustrated with money apps. Most wanted me to type everything in by hand, couldn’t handle splitting a bill with friends, got store names wrong, kept guessing at “recurring” charges and getting them wrong, and fell apart once real life got a bit complicated. So I built my own.

I use Alenjo every single day, and it’s what keeps me on top of my money. I care a lot about apps that feel simple and easy, not ones that bury you in information.

If something feels off or you have an idea, I’d love to hear it.
