From the founder

Isaiah AguilarFounder · environmental engineer

Hi, I’m Isaiah. I’m an environmental engineer who’s a little obsessed with personal finance.

I kept getting frustrated with money apps. Most wanted me to type everything in by hand, couldn’t handle splitting a bill with friends, got store names wrong, kept guessing at “recurring” charges and getting them wrong, and fell apart once real life got a bit complicated. So I built my own.

I use Alenjo every single day, and it’s what keeps me on top of my money. I care a lot about apps that feel simple and easy, not ones that bury you in information.

If something feels off or you have an idea, I’d love to hear it.
