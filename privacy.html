Privacy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Alenjo is a private, personal finance app. It connects to your bank through Plaid so you can see balances and spending in one place. This page lists exactly what it stores, where, and who else is involved.

What Alenjo stores

  • Profile: your display name and, if you add one, a profile photo.
  • Accounts: names, types, last four digits and balances for the accounts you connect.
  • Transactions: merchant, amount, date and category, plus your own edits (splits, new categories, renamed or ignored transactions, manual transactions).
  • Investments: holdings such as ticker, shares and value, when your brokerage shares them.
  • Credit cards: APR, minimum payment, due date and statement balance, when your bank shares them, plus any sign-up bonus goal you set.
  • Card designs: card images you upload to personalize your accounts.

What Alenjo does not collect

  • Your bank username or password. You enter those in Plaid's window, never in Alenjo.
  • Analytics, page views, click tracking or session recordings. There are none.
  • Advertising data. There are no ads and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Cookies. Alenjo does not set any.
  • Face ID data. Face ID happens on your device; Alenjo only stores the public part of your passkey.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored in Supabase (a PostgreSQL database). Every table uses row-level security, so each signed-in user can only read their own rows. Account names, balances and bank connection keys are also encrypted inside the database. Profile photos and card designs are kept in Supabase storage.

On your device

Alenjo keeps a few settings in your browser's local storage: your theme, whether to include pending charges, whether to ask for Face ID on open, the screen you last used, and your email and first name so the sign-in screen can greet you. Tap Not you? on the sign-in screen to clear your email and name. No balances or transactions are stored on your device.

Services Alenjo uses

  • Plaid: connects to your bank and provides balances, transactions, investments and card details (read-only).
  • Supabase: database, sign-in and the server functions that talk to Plaid.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app's files.
  • logo.dev: bank logos. Only the bank's web address is sent.
  • Google Fonts: the Inter and Newsreader typefaces. Google may see your IP address when they load.
  • jsDelivr: only when you upload a card design, it serves an image-safety model that runs on your device. Your image is not uploaded anywhere for that check.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays while your account exists. Disconnecting a bank in Settings → Banks deletes that bank's accounts, transactions and your edits to them, and asks Plaid to revoke access. To delete your whole account, email the address below.
